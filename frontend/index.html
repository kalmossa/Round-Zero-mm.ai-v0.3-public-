MM.AI Methodology

Proprietary base Elo

Elo recomputed after every fight, weighted by age, recent form, opposition quality and inactivity. Scale 1000-2500.

Scouter Rating (0-100)

Composite score out of 100: 35% base Elo, 30% career quality, 20% external ratings, 15% technical skills.

Multi-source analytics

Fight records compiled and cross-validated from multiple public MMA references. Stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase.
